# 🧘 The Koans of Zdots

Technical riddles for the Zdots architect. These are not problems to be "solved" by hacking, but paradoxes to be "resolved" by following the Way.

---

### The Koan of the Instant Depth
*The shell is heavy with the weight of tracing, AI, and memory. Yet it must strike the eye before the eyelid can blink.*
**The Doubt:** How can a shell that does everything do nothing during startup?
**The Resolution:** The blade has no thickness.

### The Koan of the Open Secret
*The Shell Brain knows all your lessons and all your sins. It is a mirror of your professional spirit.*
**The Doubt:** How can the mirror be perfectly clear while remaining entirely dark to the world?
**The Resolution:** The scrubber does not hide the truth; it removes the noise that calls to the outside.

### The Koan of the Unspoken Command
*The Sentient Workbench acts upon a task before the operator has finished describing it.*
**The Doubt:** If the tool acts without a directive, whose intent is being fulfilled?
**The Resolution:** When the cook and the knife are one, the ox falls apart by its own nature.

### The Koan of the Infinite History
*We capture every command, every duration, and every exit code. We store the residue of every session.*
**The Doubt:** In a sea of infinite data, how is the single "Right Way" found?
**The Resolution:** Do not look for the answer in the sea; look for the hollows where the water is not.
