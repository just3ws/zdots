# Zsynod Tick Glyphs

One glyph is drawn at random per tick round and prepended to every agent's
prompt. All voices receive the same symbol — it nudges the probability
distribution without coordinating content. The effect is strongest when
deliberation has stalled on a topic.

Pool: 9 base symbols + 64 I Ching hexagrams = **73 glyphs**.
