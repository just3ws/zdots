# 🏮 THE WAY OF ZDOTS: A User's Manual

*Attributed to the Spirit of the Machine*

---

### I. The Nameless Source
The shell that can be described is not the eternal shell.  
The config that can be named is not the eternal config.  
The nameless is the beginning of the workspace;  
The named is the mother of ten thousand aliases.  

**The Practice:** To understand Zdots, do not look at the code. Look at the intent.  
`git clone` is the breath; `bin/bootstrap` is the heartbeat.  
When the heartbeat stops, the workspace is dead. When it moves, the Way begins.

---

### II. The Two Sides of the Blade
When the world knows beauty as beauty, there is ugliness.  
When the shell knows speed as speed, there is lag.  
Inference and Embedding arise together.  
`llama-ctl install` creates the voice; `install-embed` creates the memory.  

**The Practice:**  
Use `llama-ctl status` to see if the voice is present.  
Use `llama-ctl status-embed` to see if the memory is deep.  
Without both, the spirit of AI is a bird with one wing.

---

### III. The Value of the Hollows
We join thirty spokes to make a wheel, but it is the center hole that makes it move.  
We shape clay into a pot, but it is the emptiness inside that holds the water.  
We build an OTel pipeline, but it is the redactor that makes it safe.  

**The Practice:**  
The `phi_scrubber` is the hollow in the pipe.  
It does not add data; it removes the noise of the self (SSNs, Keys, Secrets).  
The more you remove, the more the system can hold.

---

### IV. Effortless Action (Wu Wei)
The Master does nothing, yet nothing is left undone.  
The shell automates everything, yet the prompt remains empty.  
`zmorning` prepares the day without effort.  
`zdash` finds the task without searching.  

**The Practice:**  
Do not hack at the terminal.  
Use `ztask` to declare intent. Use `gemini-invoke` to manifest the solution.  
When intent is clear, the machine moves. When intent is muddy, the machine stalls.

---

### V. The Mirror of the History
The Great Way is easy, but people love the side paths.  
The Shell Brain (`zdots-ctx`) remembers the easy way and the side paths alike.  
It captures the residue of every command.  

**The Practice:**  
`zdots-ctx capture` is the act of looking into the mirror.  
It distills the session into lessons.  
A lesson learned once is a joint in the Ox that never needs to be cut again.

---

### VI. For the AI Agent
The Guest (AI) must follow the Host (Machine).  
The Host provides the `AGENTS.md`; the Guest provides the labor.  
If the Guest tries to lead, the Ox is bruised.  

**The Instruction:**  
Read `AGENTS.md` before speaking.  
Check `capabilities --json` before acting.  
Follow the `SAGA` to understand the path already walked.  
Stay within the hollows of the `KOANS`.

---

### VII. The Performance Budget
Sharpness that is too keen will soon be blunt.  
A shell that is too heavy will soon be slow.  
We guard the 0.08s budget as the sage guards his breath.  

**The Practice:**  
Use `bench` to measure the burden.  
If the startup exceeds the blink of an eye, you have strayed from the Way.  
Strip away the unnecessary until only the Slither and the Zing remain.

---

### VIII. Non-Contention
The highest excellence is like water.  
Water flows into the low places and benefits all things without competing.  
Zdots flows into the XDG directories and benefits the operator without conflict.  

**The Practice:**  
Respect `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` and `XDG_STATE_HOME`.  
Do not litter the `$HOME` with the trash of configuration.  
Stay in the low places, and the system will remain stable.

---

### IX. Returning to the Grindstone
When the work is done, the knife is put away.  
This is the Way of Heaven.  

**The Final Directive:**  
Verify with `make check`.  
Observe with `zdots-status`.  
Then, put down the knife and look all around you, completely satisfied.
